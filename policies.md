# Sample store policies

DEMO CONTENT ONLY. These are fictional policies for testing Policy Desk. Replace this document with your actual store policies before using it with customers. The assistant cannot access orders or perform refunds or cancellations.

## Refunds
After a returned item is received and inspected, approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Bank processing can affect when the credit appears. Shipping fees are not refundable unless the store made an error. For the status of a specific refund, contact the store's support team through the official store support channel.

## Returns
Return requests must be made within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. Final-sale and personalized items cannot be returned unless defective. Contact store support to request return instructions before sending anything back.

## Shipping
Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Shipping costs are shown at checkout. Contact store support for a specific shipment's tracking or delivery status. No international shipping policy has been provided.

## Cancellations
An order can be cancelled before dispatch by contacting store support. After dispatch, it cannot be cancelled; the return policy applies instead. This assistant cannot change or cancel orders.

## Damaged items
Report damaged or incorrect items to store support within 48 hours of delivery. Keep the packaging and provide photos through the official support channel. Support will review the issue and explain the available replacement or refund options.

## Support
Use the official store support channel for account, order, payment, or refund-status questions. This demo does not specify an email address, telephone number, working hours, or support URL. Never share card numbers or passwords in this chat.
